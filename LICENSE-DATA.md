International (CC BY 4.0). The full legal text is in
[LICENSE-CC-BY-4.0.txt](LICENSE-CC-BY-4.0.txt).

Attribute CryptoFrontierAtlas and the contributors identified in the release,
link to the release and license, retain the original scholarly citations, and
identify your changes. Record identifiers, data revisions and schema versions make attribution
and corrections traceable.

## Material with separate rights

- Cited papers, books, quotations, external websites, certificates and third-party

- Software, including schemas, tooling and synthetic software-test fixtures, is
  covered by [Apache-2.0](LICENSE). An external formalization retains its stated
